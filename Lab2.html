About Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is one of the greatest cricketers of the modern era, known for his aggressive style, unmatched consistency, and fierce passion for the game. Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, Kohli rose through the ranks with sheer determination and an unyielding spirit.
